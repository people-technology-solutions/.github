# People Technology Solutions

**Unify people, assets and data in one platform.**

People builds an intelligent platform for high-performing, sustainable logistics. It gives operations teams real-time visibility, automation and asset tracking from end to end, so cargo, containers and fleets stay visible, transparent and traceable 24/7.

## What we build

- **People Omni**: the central platform and dashboard for logistics operations
- **Modules**: connected devices for the physical side of the supply chain, including smart locks (F-Lock, P-Lock), container and trailer tracking, cameras, sound alarms and Vehicle IQ
- **Solutions**: AI route planning, task automation, temperature and humidity monitoring, tamper alerts and compliance tracking

We serve teams in transportation and logistics, FMCG, food and beverages, pharmaceuticals, retail, agriculture, construction and high-value goods.
